Our Passion is El Salvador

We were born with a clear mission: to prove that to have unforgettable experiences in the land of volcanoes you don't need a fortune, but the right guide.

Who are we?

Ámonos is more than a travel guide; it is a project created by travelers, for travelers. We know that planning a trip can be stressful, especially when you're trying to make every cent count.

We are dedicated to curating the best destinations, from the secret beaches of La Libertad to the most picturesque towns of the Flower Route, always focusing on value for money.

Our Pillars

Accessibility

We believe that traveling is a right, not a luxury. We optimize every cost so that anyone can explore.

Authenticity

We don't just take you to tourist spots, but to the real experiences that make the country vibrate.

Local Love

We promote support for local entrepreneurs and businesses in every department.
